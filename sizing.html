Prestained or unfinished

Prestained: put the stain brand, type and color on the order form and the beams arrive finished and ready to hang.

Unfinished: raw reclaimed-look texture, ready for your painter or finisher to match the house on site.

Freight from Oklahoma

The order form estimates freight from your delivery ZIP code, based on distance from Norman, Oklahoma, and the size of the order. Check the liftgate box if the jobsite has no dock or forklift. Freight is confirmed before you pay.

How ordering works

  1. Enter contact and company, beam size, lengths, finish and delivery ZIP on the order form.
  2. See the freight estimate as you go, then press Place order.
  3. We confirm freight and your ship or pickup date, then send a secure payment link. No card is charged on the order page.

Builder FAQ

Sizing questions, answered.

What box beam sizes can I order?

Heights and widths of 4, 6, 8, 10 or 12 inches, with lengths up to 24 feet per piece. You can order several lengths on one order. Sizes larger than 12 inches are quoted on request.

How are the beams built?

Every beam is a three-sided, hollow, reclaimed-look box beam, open on top for mounting. See the install guide for receiving, mounting, cuts and finish care on a job.

What finish options are there?

Prestained to your spec, giving us the stain brand, type and color, or unfinished, ready for your painter or finisher to match on site.

How is the price figured?

Trade pricing is per linear foot, by beam size and finish. The order form shows an estimate, and the final quote is confirmed before you pay.

How is freight calculated?

The order form estimates freight from your delivery ZIP code, based on distance from Norman, Oklahoma, and the size of the order. Beams ship as crated LTL freight anywhere in the lower 48, with local delivery within about 100 road miles of Norman or pickup near Norman.

How do I order?

Fill out the order form with your contact and company, beam size, lengths, finish and delivery ZIP, then press Place order. We confirm freight and send a secure payment link. No card is charged on the order page.
