Order guide

How a trade box beam order works, start to delivery.

A practical overview for home builders, GCs and remodelers, from the details you gather to the day the beams ship or are picked up. Linear feet are in the takeoff guide. Sizes are in the sizing guide. Stain is in the finish guide. Delivery is in the delivery guide. Bid wording is in the bid spec guide. Receiving and mounting are in the install guide. Terms are in the glossary.

This page walks through a trade order from the form to delivery. Pricing is quote-based. There is no public price list on this page. It is not a stamped specification. These beams are decorative and non-structural.

On this page: Before you order · The order form · After you submit · Payment · Shipping or pickup

  1. Before you order

    Have the job details in hand before you open the order form.

    • Contact name, company, email, and phone
    • Height and width for the line. The size list is in the sizing guide.
    • Piece lengths and quantities. How to figure the linear feet is in the takeoff guide.
    • Prestained, with the stain brand, type, and color, or unfinished. How to choose is in the finish guide.
    • A delivery address and ZIP, or pickup. How an order ships is in the delivery guide.
    • A liftgate if the jobsite has no dock or forklift
    • Any deadline, for the notes box on the order form
    • The bid list, if you are still writing it. What to list is in the bid spec guide.

  2. Fill in the order form

    On the order form, enter your contact and company, then the beam line.

    Set the height and the width. Each is 4, 6, 8, 10, or 12 inches. Larger than 12 inches is quoted on request. Note that size on the order. Sizes are in the sizing guide.

    Each piece can be up to 24 feet. Under Lengths, add a row for each piece length and the quantity of that length. The form totals the linear feet. Add another length for more rows of the same size and finish. Builders can put every length for a job on one order.

    Use one line per size or finish. The height, the width, and the finish on the form apply to every length row. A different height, width, or finish is its own line. Put a second size or finish in the notes. How to figure the linear feet is in the takeoff guide.

    Choose prestained or unfinished. On a prestained line, enter the stain brand, the stain type (for example oil-based, water-based, gel, semi-transparent or solid) and the color name or number. How to enter the stain is in the finish guide.

    Enter the delivery address and ZIP. Choose freight, local delivery, or pickup. Check the liftgate box, or ask for a liftgate, if the jobsite has no dock or forklift. How delivery works is in the delivery guide.

    Put any deadline in the notes box. For repeat work, mention your earlier order or job name in the notes so we can match sizes and finish.

    The order form shows an estimate as you fill it in. It estimates freight from the delivery ZIP and the size of the order. Shipping is an estimate until it is confirmed, which happens before you pay. Pricing is per linear foot by size and finish and is quote-based. There is no public price list on this page.

    Press Place order.

  1. After you submit

    Placing an order sends it to us. We confirm the quote, including freight and the ship or pickup date.

    Lead time depends on beam size, quantity and finish. The ship or pickup date is confirmed with the quote. You get a confirmed ship or pickup date with your order confirmation.

    The order form shows an estimate. The final quote and freight are confirmed before payment.

  2. How payment works

    No card is charged on the order page. We confirm freight and the ship or pickup date, then send a secure payment link.

    Questions go to wholesalebuilderbeam@agentmail.to. You can also email wholesalebuilderbeam@agentmail.to with the job details. We use the same inbox for order follow-ups.

  1. Shipping or pickup

    Beams are cut to your lengths, then prestained to your spec or left unfinished.

    They go out by freight, local delivery, or pickup. How an order ships, including a liftgate, is in the delivery guide.

    When the shipment arrives, receiving and mounting are in the install guide. Prestained beams arrive finished and ready to hang, so protect the finished faces during handling.
