Finish planning guide

Should you order prestained or unfinished box beams.

A practical overview for home builders, GCs and remodelers choosing a finish and writing it on the order. Sizes are in the sizing guide. Receiving, mounting, cuts and finish care are in the install guide. What to list on the bid is in the bid spec guide.

This page is guidance for choosing and ordering the finish. It is not a finish sample, a color-match guarantee, or a stamped specification.

On this page: The two choices · Prestained · Unfinished · How to specify · On the job · Lead time · How to order

The two finish choices

On this order, the finish is one of two choices: prestained to a stain spec, or unfinished.

The beam, in brief, is a three-sided, hollow, reclaimed-look box beam, open on top for mounting. It is decorative and non-structural.

Height and width are 4, 6, 8, 10 or 12 inches. Lengths are up to 24 feet per piece. You can mix sizes and lengths on one order. Larger than 12 inches is quoted on request.

This page does not cover structure, wrapping or mounting.

When to choose prestained

Choose prestained when you already know the stain brand, type and color and want beams finished and ready to hang.

When to choose unfinished

Choose unfinished when your painter or finisher will match the house on site. Unfinished ships as raw reclaimed-look texture.

How to specify a prestain

On the order form, pick Prestained and enter the stain brand, the stain type (for example oil-based, water-based, gel, semi-transparent or solid) and the color name or number.

We finish the beams to that spec.

On the job

Prestained beams arrive finished and ready to hang, so protect the finished faces during handling, including while pieces are carried, stacked, cut and lifted into place.

Unfinished beams stay unfinished until the painter or finisher matches the house on site.

Receiving, mounting, cuts and finish care are in the install guide.

Lead time

Lead time depends on beam size, quantity and finish. The ship or pickup date is confirmed with the quote. If the job has a deadline, put it in the notes box on the order form.

How to order
