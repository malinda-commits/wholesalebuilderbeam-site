Delivery planning guide

How decorative box beams get to your jobsite.

A practical overview for home builders, GCs and remodelers planning freight, local delivery or pickup. Sizes are in the sizing guide. Receiving, mounting and cuts are in the install guide. What to list on the bid is in the bid spec guide. Choosing prestained or unfinished is in the finish guide.

This page explains how an order gets to the jobsite. Freight and the ship or pickup date are confirmed before you pay. It is not a freight rate sheet or a stamped specification.

On this page: How it ships · What to have ready · Freight estimate · On arrival · Lead time · How to order

How an order ships

Orders ship from Norman, Oklahoma.

Beams ship as crated LTL freight anywhere in the lower 48 states. Local delivery is within about 100 road miles of Norman, and pickup is near Norman.

Lengths are up to 24 feet per piece. Heights and widths are 4, 6, 8, 10 or 12 inches, which is the context for crate size. Larger than 12 inches is quoted on request. The size list is in the sizing guide.

What to have ready

Enter the delivery address and ZIP on the order form.

Check the liftgate box, or ask for a liftgate, if the jobsite has no dock or forklift.

How the freight estimate works

The order form estimates freight from the delivery ZIP, based on distance from Norman and the size of the order. Shipping is an estimate until it is confirmed, which happens before you pay.

When the beams arrive

Prestained beams arrive finished and ready to hang, so protect the finished faces during handling.

Receiving, mounting and cuts are in the install guide.

Lead time

Lead time depends on beam size, quantity and finish. The ship or pickup date is confirmed with the quote. Put any deadline in the notes box on the order form.

How to order

Wholesale Builder Beam is trade only. We sell to home builders, general contractors and remodelers.

Pricing is per linear foot by size and finish and is quote-based. There is no public price list on this page. No card is charged on the order page. We confirm freight and the ship or pickup date, then send a secure payment link.

Builders can put every length for a job on one order.

Use the order form. Enter the delivery address and ZIP, along with your contact and company, beam size, lengths and finish.

Questions go to wholesalebuilderbeam@agentmail.to.
