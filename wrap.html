Wrap planning guide

How to wrap an existing beam with a decorative box beam.

A practical overview for home builders, GCs and remodelers who want to cover an existing beam line for appearance. Sizes are in the sizing guide. Receiving, mounting, cuts and finish care are in the install guide. What to list on the bid is in the bid spec guide.

On this page: What wrapping means · Measure first · How the shell goes on · Finish · Delivery and ordering

What wrapping means here

Wrapping, on this page, means covering an existing beam line or framing with a three-sided open-top decorative shell for appearance.

The shell is a three-sided, hollow, reclaimed-look box beam, open on top for mounting. It gives the look of a solid timber without the weight of solid stock.

The existing structure stays load-bearing. The framing above the shell, or the existing member being covered, stays the structure. The beam is the finished look.

These beams are decorative and non-structural. They wrap or hang below framing for appearance and are not load-bearing members. Do not spec or use them as structural lumber or as a replacement for a load-bearing member.

Measure the beam line first

Measure the existing beam or beam line, then choose a height and a width that will cover it. Heights and widths are 4, 6, 8, 10 or 12 inches. The size list is on the sizing guide.

If a dimension is larger than 12 inches, it is quoted on request. Note it on the order form.

Each piece can be up to 24 feet. You can mix sizes and lengths on one order.

Measure each run end to end along the finished ceiling or beam line. List every length. Order enough linear feet to cover each run. Note walls, soffits or breaks so pieces can be cut or butted on site.

How the shell goes on

The beam is open on top so it fits over a wood cleat or blocking fastened to the ceiling or beam line. The hollow shell hides the cleat once the beam is set and fastened from the sides or top returns.

Hanging details, including receiving, cuts and finish care, are in the install guide. This page does not set fastener size or spacing. Follow the job's framing, local code, and the job's details.

Finish for a wrap

Choose prestained or unfinished when you order.

Prestained. Give the stain brand, the stain type (oil-based, water-based, gel, semi-transparent or solid) and the color name or number. Beams arrive finished and ready to hang. Protect the finished faces during handling.

Unfinished. The painter or finisher matches the house on site.
