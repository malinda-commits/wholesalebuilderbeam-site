Box Beam Selections: What to Confirm With Your Homeowner

For home builders, GCs and remodelers walking a homeowner through beam choices before a trade order. Sizes are in the sizing guide. Linear feet are in the takeoff guide. Stain is in the finish guide. What to check on the form is in the order checklist.

This page is guidance for confirming selections before a trade order. It is not a stamped specification. These beams are decorative and non-structural. Wholesale Builder Beam is trade only. We sell to home builders, general contractors and remodelers.

On this page: Why lock selections · Size · Lengths · Finish · Client sign-off

Why lock selections before ordering

Beams are built to the size on the order. They are cut to your lengths, then prestained to the spec on the order or left unfinished.

Use one line per size or finish. The height, the width, and the finish on the form apply to every length row. A different height, width, or finish is its own line. Put a second size or finish in the notes. Builders can put every length for a job on one order.

These beams are decorative and non-structural. They wrap or hang below framing for appearance and are not load-bearing members.

Size: height, width, and which rooms or runs

Height and width are each 4, 6, 8, 10 or 12 inches. Larger than 12 inches is quoted on request. Note that size on the order.

Confirm which rooms or runs get which size. Write the height and the width, then the rooms or runs that share them. Name the room in the terms already used here: a great room, a kitchen, a covered porch, dining and entry, or an open living room. A run is one beam line, end to end, along the finished ceiling. Layouts already counted here are parallel, grid or coffered, ridge plus purlins, and a perimeter.

One order line is one height, one width, and one finish. If two rooms use a different height or width, they are separate lines. Put a second size in the notes.

If a beam covers an existing beam line, measure that line and choose a listed height and width that will cover it. How to plan that is in the wrap guide. The size list is in the sizing guide.

Lengths and linear feet

List every piece length, how many pieces of that length, and the total linear feet. That is what the order uses.

Each piece can be up to 24 feet. A run longer than 24 feet is more than one piece. Break that run into pieces of 24 feet or less. The linear feet stay the length of the run.

Measure each run end to end along the finished ceiling line. Note walls, soffits or breaks so pieces can be cut or butted on site. Whether to add extra linear feet for cuts and fitting is the builder's call. This page does not set an overage percentage.

How to count the runs and figure the linear feet is in the takeoff guide.

Finish

The finish on the order is one of two choices: prestained, or unfinished.

Prestained. Write the stain brand, the stain type, and the color on the order. Stain type examples are oil-based, water-based, gel, semi-transparent or solid, plus the color name or number. The beams are finished to that spec and arrive finished and ready to hang.

Unfinished. The beams ship raw, ready for the painter or finisher.

How to choose and enter the stain is in the finish guide.

Client sign-off

Print this list and write down what the homeowner confirms before the order.

Size (height and width), and which rooms or runs
 
Lengths (piece lengths, quantities, and linear feet)
 
Finish (prestained or unfinished)
 
Stain brand, type, and color
 
Delivery address, or pickup
 

Then enter the same details on the order form: height and width, piece lengths and quantities, prestained or unfinished, and a delivery address or pickup. What to check before you submit is in the order checklist. How the form runs is in the how to order guide.
