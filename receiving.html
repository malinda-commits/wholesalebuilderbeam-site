Jobsite receiving

When your box beams arrive: a jobsite receiving guide.

A practical overview for home builders, GCs and remodelers when a crate arrives. How an order ships is in the delivery guide. The order and the confirmed quote are in the how to order guide. Mounting and cuts are in the install guide. Prestained or unfinished is in the finish guide. What to confirm before you submit is in the order checklist.

When the beams arrive, inspect the shipment and match the pieces to the lengths on the order and to the confirmed quote. Prestained beams arrive finished and ready to hang, so protect the finished faces during handling. Unfinished beams ship raw, ready for your painter or finisher. If the crate is damaged or a piece does not match, note it with the driver and email wholesalebuilderbeam@agentmail.to with the order details.

This page is a jobsite receiving overview for a trade order. It is not a stamped specification. These beams are decorative and non-structural. There is no public price list on this page.

On this page: Before the truck · Check the lines · Handling · If something looks off

Before the truck shows up

The order names a delivery address and ZIP, or pickup. How an order ships is in the delivery guide.

Ask for a liftgate, or check the liftgate box, if the jobsite has no dock or forklift.

The ship or pickup date is confirmed with the quote. You get a confirmed ship or pickup date with your order confirmation. How that confirmation works is in the how to order guide.

Checking the order against your lines

Inspect the shipment on arrival. Match the pieces to the lengths on the order. Compare those pieces with the confirmed quote.

Use one line per size or finish. The height, the width, and the finish on the form apply to every length row. A different height, width, or finish is its own line. How the form is filled in is in the how to order guide.

Each piece can be up to 24 feet.

Prestained or unfinished follows that line. Prestained beams are finished to the stain brand, type and color on the order and arrive finished and ready to hang. Unfinished beams ship raw, as reclaimed-look texture. What to confirm before you submit is in the order checklist.

Handling prestained and unfinished beams

Prestained beams arrive finished and ready to hang, so protect the finished faces during handling, including while pieces are carried, stacked, cut and lifted into place.

Unfinished beams ship raw, as reclaimed-look texture, ready for your painter or finisher to match the house on site. They stay unfinished until the painter or finisher matches the house on site.

How to choose the finish is in the finish guide. Receiving, mounting and cuts are in the install guide.

If something looks off

If the crate is damaged or a piece does not match the order, note it with the driver and email wholesalebuilderbeam@agentmail.to with the order details.

Include height, width, piece lengths, finish, delivery ZIP and any deadline. Questions go to the same address.
