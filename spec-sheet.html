Box beam spec sheet for builder bids.

A printable product summary for home builders, GCs and remodelers to attach to a bid or submittal. What to list on the bid is in the bid spec guide. Sizes are in the sizing guide. Linear feet are in the takeoff guide. How a trade order runs is in the how to order guide.

This sheet is a printable product summary a builder can attach to a bid or submittal. It restates the box beam facts already on this site: what the beam is, the sizes, the finish, how it is ordered, how it ships, and how payment works.

This sheet is not a stamped specification or an engineer letter. These beams are decorative and non-structural. There is no public price list on this page.

Product at a glance

Decorative product facts only. These box beams are not load-bearing members.
Form A three-sided, hollow, reclaimed-look wood beam, open on top for mounting. It gives the look of a solid timber beam without the weight of solid stock. Decorative and non-structural. It wraps or hangs below framing for appearance and is not a load-bearing member.
Sizes Height and width are each 4, 6, 8, 10 or 12 inches. Larger than 12 inches is quoted on request. The size list is in the sizing guide.
Max piece length Each piece can be up to 24 feet. A run longer than 24 feet is more than one piece. How to split a run is in the takeoff guide.
Finish options Prestained or unfinished. Prestained means the beams are finished to the stain brand, type and color on the order and arrive finished and ready to hang. Stain type examples are oil-based, water-based, gel, semi-transparent or solid, plus the color name or number. Unfinished means the beams ship raw, as reclaimed-look texture, ready for your painter or finisher to match the house on site.
How it's ordered Trade only. Wholesale Builder Beam sells to home builders, general contractors and remodelers. Pricing is per linear foot by size and finish and is quote-based. Use one line per size or finish. The order form totals the linear feet from the piece lengths and quantities. There is no public price list on this page. How the order runs is in the how to order guide.
How it ships Beams are cut to your lengths, then prestained to your spec or left unfinished. They go out by crated LTL freight, local delivery, or pickup. Ask for a liftgate if the jobsite has no dock or forklift.
Payment step No card is charged on the order page. We confirm freight and the ship or pickup date, then send a secure payment link. The order form shows an estimate. The final quote and freight are confirmed before payment.

Not included / builder's call

These beams are decorative and non-structural. They wrap or hang below framing for appearance and are not load-bearing members. Do not spec or use them as structural lumber or as a replacement for a load-bearing member. The existing structure stays load-bearing.

Fasteners and install follow the job's framing, local code, and the job's details. The beam is open on top so it fits over a wood cleat or blocking fastened to the ceiling or beam line. This sheet does not set fastener size or spacing.

Overage is extra linear feet a builder may add for cuts and fitting. Whether to add extra, and how much, is the builder's call. This sheet does not set an overage percentage. How that choice sits on a takeoff is in the takeoff guide.
