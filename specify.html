Bid spec and takeoff

What to put on the bid for decorative box beams.

Guidance for home builders, GCs and remodelers on what to list on a takeoff or bid before ordering. Measuring each run is in the sizing guide. How the beams go up is in the install guide. Other builder questions are in the FAQ.

This page is guidance for bidding and ordering. It is not a stamped specification or an engineer letter. These beams are decorative and non-structural. Do not spec them as structural lumber.

On this page: What you are specifying · Size and lengths · Finish · Delivery · Line items · Quote and order

What you are specifying

You are specifying a three-sided, hollow, reclaimed-look box beam, open on top for mounting. It gives the look of a solid timber without the weight of solid stock.

These beams are decorative and non-structural. They wrap or hang below framing for appearance and are not load-bearing members. Do not list them on the bid as structural lumber, a load-bearing beam, or a framing member. The framing above them stays the structure. The beam is the finished look.

The size list and spec sheet are on the sizing guide. Receiving, mounting, cuts and finish care are in the install guide. How to plan wrapping an existing beam line is in the wrap guide.

Size, lengths and linear feet

On the takeoff, list the height and the width. Each is 4, 6, 8, 10 or 12 inches. Each piece can be up to 24 feet. You can mix sizes and lengths on one order. Larger than 12 inches is quoted on request; note it on the order.

Also list every piece length, how many pieces of that length, and the total linear feet. That is the quantity the order form uses.

How to measure each run end to end along the finished ceiling line, including walls, soffits or breaks, is in the sizing guide and the builder FAQ. This page is the list that goes on the bid once those lengths are known.

Finish on the bid

Choose prestained or unfinished, and write it on the takeoff.

Prestained. Give the stain brand, the stain type (oil-based, water-based, gel, semi-transparent or solid) and the color name or number. Beams arrive finished and ready to hang.

Unfinished. The beams ship raw, ready for your painter or finisher to match the house on site.

How to choose and enter the stain is in the finish guide.

Delivery address or pickup

The takeoff needs a delivery address or pickup. Freight is from Norman, Oklahoma. Beams ship as crated LTL freight anywhere in the lower 48. Local delivery is within about 100 road miles of Norman, and pickup is near Norman.

Ask for a liftgate if the site has no dock or forklift. The order form estimates freight from the delivery ZIP, based on distance from Norman, Oklahoma, and the size of the order. Shipping is an estimate until it is confirmed, which happens before you pay.

Line items to copy onto the bid

  • Height: 4, 6, 8, 10 or 12 inches
  • Width: 4, 6, 8, 10 or 12 inches
  • Each piece length, up to 24 feet, and how many pieces of that length
  • Total linear feet
  • Finish: prestained, with stain brand, type and color, or unfinished
  • Delivery address, or pickup near Norman, Oklahoma
  • Liftgate if the site has no dock or forklift
  • A deadline in the notes, if the job has one

Larger than 12 inches is quoted on request. Note that size on the order instead of forcing it into the standard list.
