Builder FAQ

Do you sell box beams at wholesale or contractor pricing?

Yes. Wholesale Builder Beam is trade only: we sell to home builders, general contractors and remodelers. Pricing is per linear foot by beam size and finish. Fill out the order form to get an estimate, and we confirm your quote before you pay.

What box beam sizes can I order?

Choose a height and width of 4, 6, 8, 10 or 12 inches, with lengths up to 24 feet per piece. You can add as many lengths as the job needs in one order. Need something larger than 12 inches? Note it on the order form and we will quote it.

Can I get beams prestained to match my stain?

Yes. Choose Prestained and enter the stain brand, the stain type (oil-based, water-based, gel, semi-transparent or solid) and the color name or number, and we finish the beams to match.

Can I order unfinished box beams?

Yes. Choose Unfinished on the order form and the beams ship raw, ready for your painter or finisher.

What is the lead time on an order?

Lead time depends on beam size, quantity and finish. You get a confirmed ship or pickup date with your order confirmation. If you have a deadline, put it in the notes box on the order form.

How is shipping calculated?

The order form estimates freight from your delivery ZIP code, based on distance from Norman, Oklahoma, and the size of your order. Check the liftgate box if the jobsite has no loading dock or forklift. Freight is an estimate until we confirm the final freight quote, which happens before you pay.

Do you ship to jobsites, or offer local delivery and pickup?

Yes to all three. We ship crated LTL freight anywhere in the lower 48 states, offer local delivery within about 100 road miles of Norman, Oklahoma, and offer pickup near Norman.

Do you handle bulk and repeat orders?

Yes. Builders can put every length for a job on one order, and larger orders may qualify for volume pricing. For repeat work, mention your earlier order or job name in the notes so we can match sizes and finish.

Can I buy for resale without sales tax?

If you are buying for resale, enter your resale or sales-tax permit number and issuing state on the order form, or tell us you will send your resale certificate. Sales tax is handled on the final invoice.

How do I place an order?

Use the order form on this page: enter your contact and company, beam size, lengths, finish and delivery details, then press Place order. We confirm freight and then send you a secure payment link. No card is charged on the order page. Questions? Email wholesalebuilderbeam@agentmail.to.
